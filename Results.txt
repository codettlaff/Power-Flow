True versus Prediction Scatterplot Results
Main issue is regression towards the center of the distribution.
At upper end, predictions get closer to the true values.

Common causes of prediction compression:
1. Training distribution lacks extreme cases. Solution: deliberately oversample tails.
2. MSE encourages conditional-mean predictions. Solutions: Weighted MSE, Huber Loss.
3. Dataset imbalance - most training examples occupy relatively narrow region of operating space.
