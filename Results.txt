Concern: Model that always guesses the average operating point could perform reasonably well.
Coefficient of determination R^2 = how much better model is than simply predicting the mean.
-- R^2 = 1 --> Perfect prediction.
-- R^2 = 0 --> No better than predicting the mean.
-- R^2 < 0 --> Worse than predicting the mean.

Results are good for every variable except V.
This is likely due to the scale of the loss.
Model minimizes (V, Theta, P, Q) simultaneously, but quantities have different scales.
Loss is dominated by errors in P and Q, optimizer has little incentive to reduce voltage error.

Solution: Normalize both inputs are targets before training. 
Train on per-unit data.
V already available in per-unit, leave as-is.
P, Q: Convert to per-unit.
For Theta, convert to radians.
